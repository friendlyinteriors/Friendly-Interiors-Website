A little clarity before you begin

Your questions,
answered.

What types of spaces do you design?

We work on residential, commercial and hospitality interiors, with services covering design, space planning, furnishing, renovation and fit-out.

Can you help with an existing space?

Yes. Whether you want to refresh the finishes, rethink a layout or plan a renovation, tell us what you’d like to change so we can discuss the scope.

How much will my project cost?

Costs depend on your space, the work required and the materials and finishes you choose. Share your project details and budget with the team to discuss a tailored proposal.

How long will the work take?

Timing depends on the scope, material availability, custom items and site conditions. Share your target date so we can discuss a practical sequence and confirm timing as the scope becomes clear.

Can we discuss the project before choosing a service?

Yes. Choose “Not sure yet” in the form, or message us directly on WhatsApp. Tell us what is working, what you want to change, and any priorities you already have.

What should I prepare for our first conversation?

Your location, a few photos or measurements, inspiration images and an idea of your budget and timeline are helpful. It’s also fine to start with an idea and a conversation.
